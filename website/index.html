Free, open source, and yours to keep.

Lipik costs nothing. There's no account, no subscription and no word limit. The code is under the MIT licence, so anyone can read it, change it or build on it.

Lipik is built on Handy, a free dictation app by CJ Pais. Lipik adds a new interface, text clean-up, snippets, tone settings and meeting notes.

Questions

What do I need to run Lipik?

A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later). For the clean-up step you also need the free Ollama app with the Qwen3 8B model, which is about 5 GB. Without it, Lipik still types what you said, just without the clean-up.

Does it cost anything?

No. Lipik is free, with no subscription, no account and no word limit.

Is there a Windows version?

Not yet. Lipik runs on macOS only for now.

Which languages does it understand?

English works out of the box with the Parakeet model. For other languages, download one of the free Whisper models in Settings; together they cover 99 languages. Hinglish (Hindi and English mixed, written in Roman letters) works but is still in beta.

Why does macOS ask for Microphone and Accessibility access?

Microphone, so Lipik can hear you. Accessibility, so it can notice when you hold fn and type the text into the app you're using. What Lipik hears stays on your Mac.

Can I use a different key instead of fn?

Yes. You can pick any shortcut in Settings. Holding fn is the default because it rarely gets in the way of anything else.
