Granola alternative

Dictation and meeting notes that run on your Mac.

On 29 Oct 2026 Granola added free voice typing for Mac. Both apps now do dictation and meeting notes, both have a free plan, and neither sends a bot to your calls. The main difference is where your speech becomes text: Granola uses cloud providers; Lipik does it on your Mac, works with no internet, and is open source.

How Lipik takes notes

Lipik records two things, kept apart: your microphone and your Mac's sound. Your microphone is you. The other side is split into Speaker 1, Speaker 2 and so on, or names when the meeting makes it clear, such as someone being called on by name. Lipik then writes a short summary with the decisions and next steps.

  • Meetings in Hinglish are noticed by themselves and written in Roman letters. The first time, Lipik downloads its Hinglish speech model (about 2 GB).
  • AI apps such as Claude, Cursor and Codex can read your notes on your Mac, read-only.
  • Without headphones, Lipik removes the call's sound from your mic before writing the transcript.

More on the meeting notes page.

When Lipik goes online

Lipik needs the internet once, to download its models. After that, dictation and meeting notes work with Wi-Fi off. It also checks for updates and sends an anonymous usage count; you can turn both off in Settings. Cloud clean-up is off unless you add your own API key. Full list on the free dictation page.

When Granola may suit you better

If you take notes on Windows or a phone, or want notes shared with your team in the cloud, Granola does that today. Lipik keeps everything on one Mac.

Tell people you're recording

Because no bot joins, nobody is told about the recording automatically. Ask before you record; in many places the law requires it.

Questions

Does Granola have dictation now?

Yes. Granola added voice typing on Mac on 29 Oct 2026. It is free on every Granola plan with no word limit, and Granola says Windows and phones are coming.

What is the main difference between Lipik and Granola?

Where your speech becomes text. Granola sends your audio to its transcription and AI providers, and its dictation needs an internet connection. Lipik turns speech into text on your Mac and works with no internet once its models are downloaded. Lipik is also open source.

Does a bot join the call?

No, in neither app. Both record your computer's audio. Lipik records your microphone and your Mac's sound on your Mac.

Is Lipik free?

Yes. Dictation, meeting notes, summaries and every other feature are free, with no account and no word limit.

Where are my notes kept?

On your Mac. The transcript, summary and recordings are made and saved on your Mac, and never leave it.

Does it work in Hinglish?

Yes. Lipik notices when a meeting mixes Hindi and English and writes the transcript in Roman letters; the first time, it downloads its Hinglish speech model, about 2 GB, and redoes that meeting. Hinglish dictation (beta) needs macOS 26 or later and uses Apple's on-device English (India) speech engine.

Which macOS version do I need?

macOS 14.2 or later for meeting notes, on a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later).
