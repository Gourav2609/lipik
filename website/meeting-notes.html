Meeting notes

Meeting notes on your Mac, without a bot.

Lipik records your side and the other side of a call on your Mac, then writes a transcript and a short summary. Nobody new joins the meeting.

Download for Mac Use with AI apps

Free and open source. Needs macOS 14.2 or later.

How it records

Some note-taking tools add a bot to your call as an extra participant. Lipik doesn't. It listens on your Mac and records two things, kept apart:

  • your microphone, for what you say
  • your Mac's sound, for what the other people say

Because the two are kept apart, the transcript shows who said what: your lines are marked as yours, and the rest as the other people's. Lipik records the Mac's sound rather than a call app, so it works with any call you can hear on your Mac.

Transcript and summary, made on your Mac

Lipik writes the transcript with its own speech model, on your Mac. Then it writes a short summary of the meeting, with the decisions and the next steps. You can add your own notes to each meeting, and ask questions about past meetings.

The summary needs the clean-up model: Qwen3 8B through the free Ollama app, about 5 GB. It runs on your Mac too. Without it, you still get the transcript, and you can write the summary later once Ollama is set up.

Ask your AI apps about a meeting

New in Lipik 0.1.2: AI apps on your Mac can read your meeting notes through MCP, a standard way for AI apps to use tools. This works with Claude, Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Windsurf, VS Code and Gemini CLI.

Access is read-only. The apps can list, search and read your meeting notes, but they can't change anything, and they can't see your dictations.

  1. Connect your apps in Lipik's Settings, under AI apps.
  2. Open a note and choose Copy link for AI apps from its menu.
  3. Paste the link into a connected app and ask about the meeting.

ChatGPT can't run tools on your Mac. For ChatGPT, choose Copy as Markdown from the same menu and paste the note in. Keep in mind that an AI app may send what it reads to its own servers.

What you need

  • A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later)
  • macOS 14.2 or later, to record the Mac's sound
  • For the summary: the free Ollama app with Qwen3 8B, about 5 GB

Questions

Does a bot join the call?

No. Lipik records on your Mac, from your microphone and your Mac's sound. Nobody new joins the meeting.

Where are my notes kept?

On your Mac. The transcript and summary are made and saved on your Mac. Lipik has no account and sends no telemetry.

Do I need Ollama?

Only for the summary and for questions about past meetings. Without it, you still get the transcript.

Which macOS version do I need?

macOS 14.2 or later, on a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later).

Can ChatGPT read my meeting notes?

Not directly. ChatGPT can't run tools on your Mac. Open the note, choose Copy as Markdown from its menu, and paste it into ChatGPT.

Should I tell people I'm recording?

Yes. Because no bot joins, nobody is told about the recording automatically. Ask before you record; in many places the law requires it.
