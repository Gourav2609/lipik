Why it's free

It runs on your Mac

Speech to text and the clean-up both run on your own computer, so there are no servers to pay for.

It's open source

The code is under the MIT licence. Anyone can read it, change it or build on it.

Questions

Is there a paid plan?

No. Everything Lipik does is free.

What's the catch?

There isn't one. No ads and no account, and your voice stays on your Mac.

Does it cost anything to run?

Only disk space: the clean-up model is about 5 GB. Everything else is included.

How can I help?

Use it, tell a friend, and report bugs or send improvements on GitHub.
