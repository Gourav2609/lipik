Hinglish, in beta

Hinglish voice typing on Mac.

Speak Hindi and English in the same sentence. Lipik types it in Roman letters, in any app on your Mac. Hinglish support is in beta.

Download for Mac How to turn it on

Free and open source. Hinglish mode needs macOS 26 or later.

What it does

A lot of us don't talk in one language at a time. A message to a friend or a teammate often mixes both. Most dictation tools expect one language, so the Hindi words come out wrong, or the tool tries to turn them into English words.

With Hinglish mode, you hold fn and talk the way you normally do. Lipik types the whole sentence in Roman letters, the way people write Hinglish in chats. It works in any app where you can type: a chat app, your email, a document or a code editor.

You say
"Hi, aaj ka din kaisa raha?"
Lipik types
Hi, aaj ka din kaisa raha?

How it works

Lipik's own speech model is built for English. For Hinglish, Lipik also uses Apple's dictation, which runs on your Mac in macOS 26 and later. With the English (India) language, Apple's dictation writes Hindi words in Roman letters.

When you speak, both run at the same time on your Mac. If what you said reads as English, Lipik keeps its own text, so English dictation is as fast as before. If it hears Hindi words, it uses Apple's text instead.

Words you add to your Dictionary, such as names, are passed to Apple's dictation too, so they are more likely to come out right.

How to turn it on

Hinglish mode is on by default on macOS 26 and later. To check it, or to turn it back on:

  1. Open Lipik's Settings and go to Dictation.
  2. Under Speech to text, set Speech engine to Auto (Hinglish mode).
  3. Check that Apple dictation language is English (India). If Lipik says Apple's speech files aren't on this Mac yet, click Download. This needs the internet once.
  4. Keep an English-only speech model, such as the default Parakeet model. With a model for many languages, Lipik uses that model on its own.

What beta means

Hinglish mode works, but it makes more mistakes than English dictation. A few limits for now:

  • It needs macOS 26 or later. On older versions, Lipik uses its English model only.
  • Hinglish mode is for dictation. Meeting notes handle Hinglish on their own, as described below.
  • It writes Roman letters only.

If something comes out wrong, you can report it on GitHub.

Hinglish in meetings

Meeting notes don't use Hinglish mode. They notice Hindi by themselves and write the transcript in Roman letters, with Hindi words and names kept, on macOS 14.2 or later. That is covered on the Hinglish speech to text page.

Questions

Is Hinglish voice typing free?

Yes. Lipik is free and open source under the MIT licence, with no account and no word limit. Hinglish mode is included.

Which apps does it work in?

Any app where you can type: a chat app, your email, a document or a code editor. Hold fn, talk, and let go; Lipik types where your cursor is.

Which macOS version do I need?

macOS 26 or later, on a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later). On older versions of macOS, dictation works in English.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once the speech files are on your Mac. Lipik's own speech model is a one-time 731 MB download from Hugging Face. Apple's English (India) speech files are a one-time download through macOS, if your Mac doesn't have them yet. After that, Hinglish voice typing works with Wi-Fi off.

Does it write Hindi in Devanagari?

No. Hinglish mode writes everything in Roman letters, the way most people type Hinglish in chats.

Does Hinglish mode slow down English dictation?

No. If what you said reads as English, Lipik keeps its own text, so English dictation is as fast as before.

Why is it called beta?

It works, but it makes more mistakes than English dictation. Hindi words can come out with a different spelling, or a word can be missed. We are still improving it.
