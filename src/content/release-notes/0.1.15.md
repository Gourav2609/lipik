### Download a speech model again when yours is missing

If your speech model's files are deleted, for example by a disk clean-up, Settings → Dictation → Model now shows "Choose a model", and picking one downloads it. Before, the list looked as if a model was already chosen, and picking it did nothing.

### Clearer meeting-notes tools for AI apps

Lipik's meeting-notes tools for AI apps now tell those apps that they only read your notes and never change anything.
