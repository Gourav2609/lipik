### Redo transcript in Hinglish works

In 0.1.10, choosing **Redo transcript in Hinglish** did nothing: Lipik looked for its Hinglish speech model under the wrong name and stopped without a word. It now finds the model, downloads it the first time (about 2 GB), and redoes the meeting. On a 19-minute Hinglish call, the transcript went from about 480 garbled English words to about 2,800 words of real Hinglish.

### You can see it working

Right after you choose it, the meeting shows "Getting the Hinglish speech model… 34%", then "Redoing the transcript in Hinglish…". If something goes wrong, it says why, with Try again.

### Menus stay on top

The meeting's menu no longer slides under the details card on the right.
