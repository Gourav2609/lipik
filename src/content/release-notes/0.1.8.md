### Speakers named from the calendar invite

When someone is called on by name ("Naresh, can you go next?") or introduces themselves ("Gautam here"), Lipik puts the right name from the meeting invite on that voice. It only names a voice when the meeting makes it clear, so you get "Speaker 2" rather than a wrong name. Rooms and group addresses on the invite are skipped. Name a voice once and Lipik recognises it in every meeting after that.

### Window buttons stay in place

Switching between light and dark no longer moves the close, minimise and zoom buttons to the corner of the window.
