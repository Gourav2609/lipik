### Meeting transcripts without the echo

On calls without headphones, your microphone also hears the call from the speakers. Lipik was writing that leftover sound down as nonsense lines from you. Those lines are now left out, and long meetings finish processing sooner.

### Lipik remembers who is who

When Lipik works out a speaker's name in one meeting, it keeps that voice and names the same person in later meetings they are invited to. Hearing someone handed the floor once is now enough when the voice also matches.

### Check the names with one click

Above a transcript, each name Lipik gave on its own has a tick and a cross. The tick keeps the name for every later meeting; the cross puts the speaker back to a number.

### Names even when notes fail

Speakers are now named before the notes are written, so the notes use the names, and a meeting whose notes fail still gets them.

### The copy card is back

When you dictate with no text box in focus, Lipik shows the text with a copy button again instead of pasting it nowhere. This had stopped working in front of some apps.
