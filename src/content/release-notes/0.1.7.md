### Meetings get real titles

A call with no calendar event used to be called "Slack Call Meeting" or "Brave Call Meeting". Lipik now names it after what was discussed, like "Speech Model Setup and Troubleshooting". Titles you set, and calendar titles, stay as they are. For older meetings, choose **Regenerate** in the meeting's menu.

### No waiting for the 5 GB download in setup

Start the clean-up model download and press Continue: it keeps going in the background. Dictation works right away, and clean-up turns on by itself when the model is ready. A meeting recorded in the meantime gets its notes as soon as the download finishes.

### Lipik's own models

Every speech model now downloads from Lipik's own copies on Hugging Face.
