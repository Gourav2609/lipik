### Meetings in a room

When everyone talks into your Mac's microphone, Lipik now tells the voices apart: you, then Speaker 1, 2, 3. It knows which voice is yours from your own dictation. Turn this off with **Know my voice** in Settings → Meetings.

### Cleaner meeting transcripts

Hinglish meetings come out in Roman letters only, and numbers are never changed when Hindi is written out. Words in your Vocabulary fix what the speech model mishears, like "Zoom Info" to ZoomInfo. Repeated loops and made-up phrases in silences are removed. Lines show the time.

### Better meeting notes

Notes now keep decisions, proposals and open questions apart, copy numbers as they were said, and never invent who does what or by when.

### Press Enter after dictation

Turn on **Press Enter after dictation** in Settings → Dictation to send the message right after the text is typed, in Slack or ChatGPT for example.

### Easier first setup

Setup picks a speech model for your Mac's language, downloads it before the clean-up model, and the pill tells you if you press fn before it's ready. The Terminal installer also updates Lipik while AI apps are connected to it.

### Smaller fixes

A capital "UM" is no longer typed. Call audio no longer reconnects over and over when nothing is playing. Setup now asks whether to send the anonymous count, which also says how far setup got.
