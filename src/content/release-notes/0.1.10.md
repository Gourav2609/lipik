### Hinglish meetings caught properly

Some Hindi-and-English calls were still treated as English, which left a thin, garbled transcript. Lipik now also checks how many words come out per minute of speech: English calls give well over 100, Hinglish calls through the English model far fewer. Those meetings now switch to Hinglish on their own. For an older meeting, open its menu and choose **Redo transcript in Hinglish**.

### Long dictations finish sooner

Lipik now tidies each finished sentence while you are still talking, so a long dictation appears about three times faster after you let go of fn. The words are exactly what they were before.

### The mic name only when it changes

The pill names your microphone only when it differs from last time, for example when you connect Bluetooth headphones.
