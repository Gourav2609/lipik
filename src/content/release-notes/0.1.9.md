### Meetings in Hinglish

When a meeting mixes Hindi and English, Lipik now notices by itself and writes the transcript in Roman letters, the way you'd type Hinglish on WhatsApp. Hindi words and people's names are kept instead of turning into English-sounding noise. The first time, Lipik quietly downloads its Hinglish speech model (about 2 GB) and redoes that meeting. You can set the language yourself in Settings → Meetings, but you shouldn't need to.

### Names from hand-overs

When someone is spoken to by name, like "Mohan, any update?", and the same voice answers each time, Lipik puts that name from the invite on the voice. One mention isn't enough, so you get "Speaker 3" rather than a wrong name.

### No more call echo on your side

Without headphones your mic also hears the call, so other people's words used to show up as yours. Lipik now removes the call's sound from your mic before transcribing. Your own voice is kept as it was.
