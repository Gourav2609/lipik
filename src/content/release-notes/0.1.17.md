### The usage count now says how Lipik is used

If you left the usage count on, from this version it also sends how much you use Lipik (counts of dictations, words, meetings and meeting minutes) and five settings: the speech model, the speech engine, the language, the clean-up engine, and whether meeting notes are on. It never sends your voice, your text or any names. If you are updating, these are sent only after you close this note. To turn the count off, go to Settings → Privacy → Usage count.
