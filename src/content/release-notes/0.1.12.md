### Real names in Slack huddles

Slack marks whoever is talking in a huddle. Lipik can now read that marker while it records a huddle and put each person's Slack name on their voice, even in a first meeting where nobody says a name. It reads only the huddle tiles, never messages, and keeps the names and times with the meeting on your Mac. Turn it on in **Settings → Meetings → Names from Slack huddles**. Keep the huddle on the desktop you're using: macOS can't read windows on another desktop.

### Careful by design

A voice is named only when Slack's marker clearly matches it for at least 10 seconds. Two people sharing one laptop, or a tile that matches two voices, stay as Speaker 1 and Speaker 2. Names you set yourself always win.
