### Hinglish lines no longer cut short

On macOS 26, when the English speech model kept only the English words of a Hindi sentence ("Yard staging server." for "Yaar, staging server phir se down hai, koi dekh sakta hai kya"), Lipik now uses Apple's transcript, which heard the whole line.

### Question marks on Hinglish questions

Questions like "Khana kha liya kya?" and "Deploy aaj ho jayega na?" now end with a question mark.

### Hinglish corrections

"Main paanch baje aaunga, nahi nahi, chhe baje" is now corrected even in a short dictation. A single "nahi", as in "ya nahi", is no longer treated as a correction.

### Safer clean-up

If the clean-up model leaves out a phrase you said, Lipik types your words instead of its reply.
