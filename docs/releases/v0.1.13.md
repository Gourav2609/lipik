Voices told apart in room meetings, cleaner transcripts and notes, Press Enter after dictation, and an easier first setup.

## What's new

- **Meetings in a room.** When everyone talks into your Mac's microphone, Lipik now tells the voices apart: you, then Speaker 1, 2, 3. It knows which voice is yours from your own dictation. Turn this off with **Know my voice** in Settings → Meetings.
- **Cleaner meeting transcripts.** Hinglish meetings come out in Roman letters only, and numbers are never changed when Hindi is written out. Words in your Vocabulary fix what the speech model mishears, like "Zoom Info" to ZoomInfo. Repeated loops and made-up phrases in silences are removed. Lines show the time.
- **Better meeting notes.** Notes now keep decisions, proposals and open questions apart, copy numbers as they were said, and never invent who does what or by when.
- **Press Enter after dictation.** Turn on **Press Enter after dictation** in Settings → Dictation to send the message right after the text is typed, in Slack or ChatGPT for example.
- **Easier first setup.** Setup picks a speech model for your Mac's language, downloads it before the clean-up model, and the pill tells you if you press fn before it's ready. The Terminal installer also updates Lipik while AI apps are connected to it.
- **Smaller fixes.** A capital "UM" is no longer typed. Call audio no longer reconnects over and over when nothing is playing. Setup now asks whether to send the anonymous count, which also says how far setup got.

## Install or update

Easiest: paste this into Terminal and press Return. It installs or updates Lipik with no security warning:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://lipik.pages.dev/install.sh | sh
```

Or by hand:

1. Download **Lipik.dmg** below (Apple silicon, M1 and later) and drag **Lipik** into **Applications**, replacing the old one.
2. First time only: Lipik isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so macOS blocks the first launch.
   - **macOS 15 and later:** double-click Lipik, click **Done**, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.
   - **macOS 14 and earlier:** right-click Lipik in Applications, choose **Open**, then **Open**.
3. Updating keeps your settings, history and permissions.
