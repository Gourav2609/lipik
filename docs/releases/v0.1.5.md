One-click updates, a few small fixes, and an anonymous usage count.

## What's new

- **One-click updates.** When a new version is out, a card next to the pill says so, with **Update now**. Lipik downloads it, replaces itself and restarts, keeping your settings, history and permissions. It never restarts during a meeting. The check runs every few hours and carries nothing about you; turn it off in **Settings → Privacy → Updates**. From this version on you won't need to download Lipik again by hand.
- **Hear about new versions by email.** Setup now asks for an email address so we can tell you when a new version is out. It's optional: skip it, and nothing is sent.
- **Run setup again** from **Settings → About**, without losing your settings.
- **An anonymous usage count.** Every few hours Lipik sends a random ID, the app version and the macOS version, so we know how many people use it. Never your voice, your text or your name, and the server doesn't keep your IP address. Turn it off in **Settings → Privacy → Usage count**. Setup tells you about it before anything is sent.
- **Meeting notes scroll clear of the Ask bar**, so the last lines are no longer hidden under it.

## Install or update

Easiest: paste this into Terminal and press Return. It installs or updates Lipik with no security warning:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://lipik.refflux.dev/install.sh | sh
```

Or by hand:

1. Download **Lipik.dmg** below (Apple silicon, M1 and later) and drag **Lipik** into **Applications**, replacing the old one.
2. First time only: Lipik isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so macOS blocks the first launch.
   - **macOS 15 and later:** double-click Lipik, click **Done**, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.
   - **macOS 14 and earlier:** right-click Lipik in Applications, choose **Open**, then **Open**.
3. Updating keeps your settings, history and permissions.
