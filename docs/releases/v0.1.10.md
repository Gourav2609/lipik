## What's new

- **Hinglish meetings caught properly.** Some Hindi-and-English calls were still treated as English, which left a thin, garbled transcript. Lipik now also checks how many words come out per minute of speech: English calls give well over 100, Hinglish calls through the English model far fewer. Those meetings now switch to Hinglish on their own. For an older meeting, open its menu and choose **Redo transcript in Hinglish**.
- **Long dictations finish sooner.** Lipik now tidies each finished sentence while you are still talking, so a long dictation appears about three times faster after you let go of fn. The words are exactly what they were before.
- **The mic name only when it changes.** The pill names your microphone only when it differs from last time, for example when you connect Bluetooth headphones.

## Install or update

Easiest: paste this into Terminal and press Return. It installs or updates Lipik with no security warning:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://lipik.pages.dev/install.sh | sh
```

Or by hand:

1. Download **Lipik.dmg** below (Apple silicon, M1 and later) and drag **Lipik** into **Applications**, replacing the old one.
2. First time only: Lipik isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so macOS blocks the first launch.
   - **macOS 15 and later:** double-click Lipik, click **Done**, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.
   - **macOS 14 and earlier:** right-click Lipik in Applications, choose **Open**, then **Open**.
3. Updating keeps your settings, history and permissions.
