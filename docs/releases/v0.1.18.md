Meeting notes that arrive, note templates, saved prompts, saving a note as a file, and editing text by voice.

## What's new

- **Meeting notes arrive far more reliably.** The AI model sometimes got stuck repeating itself, and Lipik then threw the notes away. Now it keeps what was written, asks again in smaller pieces when it must, and leaves out only the stretch it could not handle, saying which minutes. Notes that still fail are tried again by themselves, 30 minutes later and at the next start. On our own meetings, the ones that had failed now get full notes, and short meetings take about half the time.
- **Note templates.** A template adds its own sections to a meeting's notes. Standup, One-to-one, Customer call and Interview come built in, and you can write your own in Settings → Meetings → Templates. Pick one for all new meetings there, or for one meeting from its menu (Notes template). A template makes the notes take a little longer.
- **Saved prompts.** Things you often ask of a meeting are one click away under its notes: Follow-up email, My to-dos, Status update and Open points come built in. Add your own in Settings → Meetings → Saved prompts.
- **Save a note as a file.** A meeting's menu has Save as Markdown: the notes, your own notes and the whole transcript in one file.
- **Edit by voice.** Select some text, hold Control+Option+Space and say what to change, like "make it shorter" or "turn this into bullet points". Lipik rewrites the selection on your Mac. Change the keys in Settings → Dictation. It is new: plain rewrites work well, translating to Hinglish does not yet.

## Install or update

Easiest: paste this into Terminal and press Return. It installs or updates Lipik with no security warning:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://lipik.pages.dev/install.sh | sh
```

Or by hand:

1. Download **Lipik.dmg** below (Apple silicon, M1 and later) and drag **Lipik** into **Applications**, replacing the old one.
2. First time only: Lipik isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so macOS blocks the first launch.
   - **macOS 15 and later:** double-click Lipik, click **Done**, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.
   - **macOS 14 and earlier:** right-click Lipik in Applications, choose **Open**, then **Open**.
3. Updating keeps your settings, history and permissions.
