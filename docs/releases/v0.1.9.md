Meetings in Hinglish, names from hand-overs, and no more call echo on your side.

## What's new

- **Meetings in Hinglish.** When a meeting mixes Hindi and English, Lipik now notices by itself and writes the transcript in Roman letters, the way you'd type Hinglish on WhatsApp. Hindi words and people's names are kept instead of turning into English-sounding noise. The first time, Lipik quietly downloads its Hinglish speech model (about 2 GB) and redoes that meeting. You can set the language yourself in Settings → Meetings, but you shouldn't need to.
- **Names from hand-overs.** When someone is spoken to by name, like "Mohan, any update?", and the same voice answers each time, Lipik puts that name from the invite on the voice. One mention isn't enough, so you get "Speaker 3" rather than a wrong name.
- **No more call echo on your side.** Without headphones your mic also hears the call, so other people's words used to show up as yours. Lipik now removes the call's sound from your mic before transcribing. Your own voice is kept as it was.

## Install or update

Easiest: paste this into Terminal and press Return. It installs or updates Lipik with no security warning:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://lipik.pages.dev/install.sh | sh
```

Or by hand:

1. Download **Lipik.dmg** below (Apple silicon, M1 and later) and drag **Lipik** into **Applications**, replacing the old one.
2. First time only: Lipik isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so macOS blocks the first launch.
   - **macOS 15 and later:** double-click Lipik, click **Done**, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.
   - **macOS 14 and earlier:** right-click Lipik in Applications, choose **Open**, then **Open**.
3. Updating keeps your settings, history and permissions.
