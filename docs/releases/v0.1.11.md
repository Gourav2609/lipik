Redo transcript in Hinglish now works and shows its progress, and menus stay on top.

## What's new

- **Redo transcript in Hinglish works.** In 0.1.10, choosing **Redo transcript in Hinglish** did nothing: Lipik looked for its Hinglish speech model under the wrong name and stopped without a word. It now finds the model, downloads it the first time (about 2 GB), and redoes the meeting. On a 19-minute Hinglish call, the transcript went from about 480 garbled English words to about 2,800 words of real Hinglish.
- **You can see it working.** Right after you choose it, the meeting shows "Getting the Hinglish speech model… 34%", then "Redoing the transcript in Hinglish…". If something goes wrong, it says why, with Try again.
- **Menus stay on top.** The meeting's menu no longer slides under the details card on the right.

## Install or update

Easiest: paste this into Terminal and press Return. It installs or updates Lipik with no security warning:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://lipik.pages.dev/install.sh | sh
```

Or by hand:

1. Download **Lipik.dmg** below (Apple silicon, M1 and later) and drag **Lipik** into **Applications**, replacing the old one.
2. First time only: Lipik isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so macOS blocks the first launch.
   - **macOS 15 and later:** double-click Lipik, click **Done**, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.
   - **macOS 14 and earlier:** right-click Lipik in Applications, choose **Open**, then **Open**.
3. Updating keeps your settings, history and permissions.
