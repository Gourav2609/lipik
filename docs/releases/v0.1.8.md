Speakers named from the calendar invite, and window buttons that stay put.

## What's new

- **Speakers named from the calendar invite.** When someone is called on by name ("Naresh, can you go next?") or introduces themselves ("Gautam here"), Lipik puts the right name from the meeting invite on that voice. It only names a voice when the meeting makes it clear, so you get "Speaker 2" rather than a wrong name. Rooms and group addresses on the invite are skipped. Name a voice once and Lipik recognises it in every meeting after that.
- **Window buttons stay in place.** Switching between light and dark no longer moves the close, minimise and zoom buttons to the corner of the window.

## Install or update

Easiest: paste this into Terminal and press Return. It installs or updates Lipik with no security warning:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://lipik.pages.dev/install.sh | sh
```

Or by hand:

1. Download **Lipik.dmg** below (Apple silicon, M1 and later) and drag **Lipik** into **Applications**, replacing the old one.
2. First time only: Lipik isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so macOS blocks the first launch.
   - **macOS 15 and later:** double-click Lipik, click **Done**, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.
   - **macOS 14 and earlier:** right-click Lipik in Applications, choose **Open**, then **Open**.
3. Updating keeps your settings, history and permissions.
