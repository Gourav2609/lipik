Ask your AI apps about your meetings, plus better emails and dictionary words.

## What's new

- **Your meetings in your AI apps.** Claude, Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Windsurf, VS Code and Gemini CLI can now read your Lipik meeting notes, on your Mac and read-only. Connect them in the new setup step or in **Settings → AI apps**. Each note's menu has **Copy link for AI apps**: paste the link into a connected app and ask about that meeting. ChatGPT can't run tools on your Mac, so use **Copy as Markdown** there.
- **"At the rate" means @.** Say "rivaan at the rate quavia dot com" and Lipik writes rivaan@quavia.com. It only does this between a name and a web address, so "interest at the rate of 7 percent" stays as you said it.
- **Your own email, spelled right.** Speech models often mangle a name inside an email ("Gorbaikitchen dot com"). Add your email to the **Dictionary** and Lipik turns anything that sounds like it into the real address. Other addresses, like a colleague's at the same company, are left alone.
- **Dictionary words that start with a different letter.** "Kwavia" now becomes "Quavia" when Quavia is in your Dictionary (c, k and q at the start sound alike).
- **Meeting reminder.** "Start meeting notes" no longer gets cut off on the card.

## Install or update

1. Download **Lipik.dmg** below (Apple silicon, M1 and later) and drag **Lipik** into **Applications**, replacing the old one.
2. First time only: Lipik isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so macOS blocks the first launch.
   - **macOS 15 and later:** double-click Lipik, click **Done**, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.
   - **macOS 14 and earlier:** right-click Lipik in Applications, choose **Open**, then **Open**.
3. Updating keeps your settings, history and permissions.
