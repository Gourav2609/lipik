Cleaner meeting transcripts, speaker names that carry over between meetings, and the copy card is back.

## What's new

- **Meeting transcripts without the echo.** On calls without headphones, your microphone also hears the call from the speakers. Lipik was writing that leftover sound down as nonsense lines from you. Those lines are now left out, and long meetings finish processing sooner.
- **Lipik remembers who is who.** When Lipik works out a speaker's name in one meeting, it keeps that voice and names the same person in later meetings they are invited to. Hearing someone handed the floor once is now enough when the voice also matches.
- **Check the names with one click.** Above a transcript, each name Lipik gave on its own has a tick and a cross. The tick keeps the name for every later meeting; the cross puts the speaker back to a number.
- **Names even when notes fail.** Speakers are now named before the notes are written, so the notes use the names, and a meeting whose notes fail still gets them.
- **The copy card is back.** When you dictate with no text box in focus, Lipik shows the text with a copy button again instead of pasting it nowhere. This had stopped working in front of some apps.

## Install or update

Easiest: paste this into Terminal and press Return. It installs or updates Lipik with no security warning:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://lipik.pages.dev/install.sh | sh
```

Or by hand:

1. Download **Lipik.dmg** below (Apple silicon, M1 and later) and drag **Lipik** into **Applications**, replacing the old one.
2. First time only: Lipik isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so macOS blocks the first launch.
   - **macOS 15 and later:** double-click Lipik, click **Done**, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.
   - **macOS 14 and earlier:** right-click Lipik in Applications, choose **Open**, then **Open**.
3. Updating keeps your settings, history and permissions.
