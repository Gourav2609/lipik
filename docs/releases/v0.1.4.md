Two dictation fixes: spaces between dictations, and no stray comma where "um" was.

## What's new

- **A space between one dictation and the next.** Dictate a sentence, press the key again and keep talking: Lipik now adds a space when the cursor sits right after other text, so the two parts no longer run together ("tomorrow.Then"). It checks the character before the cursor, so there is no extra space at the start of a line, after a space or after an opening bracket.
- **No stray comma where a filler was.** "Hey can we, um, move the call" used to paste as "Hey can we, move the call". The comma is now dropped when it sat inside a phrase, and kept where it belongs ("Hey, um, can we" becomes "Hey, can we").

## Install or update

1. Download **Lipik.dmg** below (Apple silicon, M1 and later) and drag **Lipik** into **Applications**, replacing the old one.
2. First time only: Lipik isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so macOS blocks the first launch.
   - **macOS 15 and later:** double-click Lipik, click **Done**, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.
   - **macOS 14 and earlier:** right-click Lipik in Applications, choose **Open**, then **Open**.
3. Updating keeps your settings, history and permissions.
