Spaces and full stops between dictations, a fixed first-time setup, and Lipik spells its own name.

## What's new

- **Your next dictation no longer runs into the last one.** Dictate, let go, then dictate again: Lipik puts a space between the two ("…by tomorrow. Then we…"). This now works in every app, including ones like Slack and Claude that don't tell macOS where the cursor is. It leaves the space out after you press Enter, type, or click somewhere else.
- **Sentences end with a full stop.** In email, Slack, AI chats and documents, a dictation of three or more words that stops mid-sentence now ends with a full stop. Personal chats, terminals, code editors and browser search boxes are left as you said them.
- **No stray comma where you said "um".** "Hey can we, um, move the call" now comes out as "Hey can we move the call". Commas that belong there stay.
- **Lipik spells its own name.** "Lippik", "Lippic", "lipic" and similar now come out as "Lipik".
- **Setup no longer stalls on a new Mac.** On a first install, setup could start the same speech model download many times at once and get stuck, or wait forever on "Checking the clean-up model…". Both are fixed, and the window edges now match Lipik's dark theme.
- The end of a long meeting note now scrolls clear of the "Ask about this meeting" bar.
- **Settings → About → Run setup again** walks you through setup without changing your settings.
- Lipik now downloads its speech models from its own Hugging Face copies, and every Handy name is gone from the app.

## Install or update

Easiest: paste this into Terminal and press Return. It installs or updates Lipik with no security warning:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://lipik.refflux.dev/install.sh | sh
```

Or by hand:

1. Download **Lipik.dmg** below (Apple silicon, M1 and later) and drag **Lipik** into **Applications**, replacing the old one.
2. First time only: Lipik isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so macOS blocks the first launch.
   - **macOS 15 and later:** double-click Lipik, click **Done**, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.
   - **macOS 14 and earlier:** right-click Lipik in Applications, choose **Open**, then **Open**.
3. Updating keeps your settings, history and permissions.
