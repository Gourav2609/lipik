Clean-up without Ollama, speakers in meeting notes, and a notice when a new version is out.

## What's new

- **Clean-up is built in.** You no longer need the separate Ollama app. In setup, or in **Settings → Meetings**, one button downloads the clean-up model: 5 GB on Macs with 16 GB of memory or more, 2.5 GB on smaller Macs. It runs on your Mac with the same quality as before and a little faster. Meeting summaries and Ask use it too. If you already use Ollama, nothing changes: Lipik keeps using it, and you can switch in Settings.
- **Who said what in meeting notes.** The other side of a call is now split into Speaker 1, Speaker 2 and so on, instead of one "Them". Click a speaker's name to rename them everywhere in that meeting. Lipik then recognises that voice in later meetings. Names are only filled in on clear evidence, such as someone introducing themselves. The first meeting after updating downloads a 33 MB speaker model. Calls longer than 3 hours keep a single "Them".
- **Saved voices are yours to delete.** **Settings → Privacy → Saved voices** shows how many voices Lipik remembers, with **Forget all voices**. Voices stay on your Mac and are removed with their meeting.
- **New-version notice.** Once a day Lipik asks GitHub for the latest version number. When a newer one is out, a small notice links to it. The request carries nothing about you. Turn it off in **Settings → Privacy → Updates**.
- Built-in clean-up needs macOS 13.3 or later; older Macs keep using Ollama.

## Install or update

1. Download **Lipik.dmg** below (Apple silicon, M1 and later) and drag **Lipik** into **Applications**, replacing the old one.
2. First time only: Lipik isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so macOS blocks the first launch.
   - **macOS 15 and later:** double-click Lipik, click **Done**, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.
   - **macOS 14 and earlier:** right-click Lipik in Applications, choose **Open**, then **Open**.
3. Updating keeps your settings, history and permissions.
