Meeting titles from what was said, and no waiting for the clean-up download in setup.

## What's new

- **Meetings get real titles.** A call with no calendar event used to be called "Slack Call Meeting" or "Brave Call Meeting". Lipik now names it after what was discussed, like "Speech Model Setup and Troubleshooting". Titles you set, and calendar titles, stay as they are. For older meetings, choose **Regenerate** in the meeting's menu.
- **No waiting for the 5 GB download in setup.** Start the clean-up model download and press Continue: it keeps going in the background. Dictation works right away, and clean-up turns on by itself when the model is ready. A meeting recorded in the meantime gets its notes as soon as the download finishes.
- **Lipik's own models.** Every speech model now downloads from Lipik's own copies on Hugging Face.

## Install or update

Easiest: paste this into Terminal and press Return. It installs or updates Lipik with no security warning:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://lipik.refflux.dev/install.sh | sh
```

Or by hand:

1. Download **Lipik.dmg** below (Apple silicon, M1 and later) and drag **Lipik** into **Applications**, replacing the old one.
2. First time only: Lipik isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so macOS blocks the first launch.
   - **macOS 15 and later:** double-click Lipik, click **Done**, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.
   - **macOS 14 and earlier:** right-click Lipik in Applications, choose **Open**, then **Open**.
3. Updating keeps your settings, history and permissions.
